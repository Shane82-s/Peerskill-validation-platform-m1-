How validation works

The basic flow is simple: sign up → upload a micro-project (or demo) → request peer reviews. Once peers validate your submission and the community reaches consensus, you'll earn XP and badges that reflect your verified skills.

Steps

  1. Sign up: Create an account and pick your domain(s).
  2. Upload: Submit a micro-project, repo link, or short demo video.
  3. Review: Validators score using rubrics; consensus awards verification.

XP & Badges (example)

Example thresholds: Bronze 50 XP • Silver 150 XP • Gold 300 XP. XP is earned when validators approve or give positive scores; badges unlock at thresholds.

Become a Validator

Validators review submissions, score using rubrics, and help build trust. Fill the short application to apply.

Requirements

  • At least 3 verified skills on PeerSkill.
  • Completed validator training quiz (automated).
  • Agree to the community code of ethics.

Benefits

  • Earn reputation and validation credits.
  • Access to moderation dashboards.
  • Special recognition badges.
